📋 Quiz Rules

  1. 📝 The quiz contains 10 questions in total.
  2. 🔘 Each question has three (3) options.
  3. ☝️ Select only one option for each question.
  4. ⭐ Each option has a different number of marks.
  5. 💡 The marks depend on the option you choose.
  6. 📖 Read every question carefully before answering.
  7. ➕ The marks from all your selected options are added together.
  8. 🎯 Your selected answers determine your total score.
  9. ✅ Answer all 10 questions before submitting the quiz.
  10. 🎉 Your personality category is determined by your total score.

Take your time, trust yourself, and enjoy the quiz! 💜
